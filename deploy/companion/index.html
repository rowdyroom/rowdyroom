Get BP with TikTok LIVE Gifts

Send one of these gifts during the host's TikTok LIVE. BP goes automatically to the wallet for your TikTok username. Connect that same username here to view and spend it.

  • Hand Heart: 100 BP
  • Amped Up: 274 BP
  • Drum Pop: 575 BP
  • Gold Mine: 1,200 BP

Buy/send gifts through TikTok's LIVE gift tray. Each completed gift credits the listed BP; a completed gift streak credits that amount for each gift in the streak. TikFinity must be connected. Gifts do not move your turn automatically—request a skip below for host approval.

Skip Ahead · 25 BP per position

TikTok chat: !skip 2 for yourself or !skip @friend 2 for a friend. Choose 1–3 positions; host approval required.

Move your own turn or sponsor another singer. Your wallet pays for the skip. You cannot pass the current or up-next singer.

Request a Song to Play · 25 BP

Ask the host to play a song. This does not put you in the singing rotation.

Each person can buy up to 3 positions for themselves and 3 positions for other singers per rolling hour. The host checks the requester and approves both options. BP is charged only on approval. Declined requests cost nothing.

Earn BP by Watching & Participating

  • Active TikTok viewers: 1 BP per five-minute interval with a comment or likes (up to 12 BP per hour). No Companion App required; TikFinity must be connected to the live show. Silent viewing is not tracked.
  • Accepted live vote: 1 BP (up to 12 BP per hour).
  • TikTok follow: 10 BP. Live share: 15 BP. Like milestone: 5 BP, when recorded by the show's TikTok integration.
  • Completed singing: 10 BP (up to 10 BP per hour).

Use your actual TikTok username to see rewards earned during the live show. TikTok activity rewards arrive even with this app closed.
