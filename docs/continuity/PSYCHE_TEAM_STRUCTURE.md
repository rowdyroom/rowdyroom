# Psyche Team structure

Owner-approved terminology, effective November 3, 2026.

**Psyche Team** is the combined organization. It is made up of **two independent teams**: the **Cloud Team** and the **Local Team**.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Psyche Team | Everything combined: both teams and their coordinated work |
| Cloud Team | The independent team of cloud-based AI workers |
| Local Team | The independent team of AI workers running on Roger's own equipment |
| Worker | An individual AI member of either team |
| Role | A worker's responsibility, such as manager, researcher, editor or specialist |
| Device | Equipment hosting or accessing workers; a device is not itself a team |

Each team has its own identity and assignments. The two teams may collaborate on a shared objective. Keep each team's contributions and results distinguishable; an online worker or device is not proof of useful contribution.

This request establishes the organization and vocabulary. It does not itself change runtime routing, permissions, active models, worker identities, service isolation or the existing cloud management authority. A local worker is not renamed a cloud worker merely because a cloud manager coordinates its assignment. Future implementation should name the team and individual worker separately.
